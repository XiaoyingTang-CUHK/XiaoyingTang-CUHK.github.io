Welcome to T-lab

T-Lab, led by Prof. Xiaoying Tang (used name: Wanrong Tang) at The Chinese University of Hong Kong, Shenzhen, has published high-impact work at NeurIPS, ICML, ICLR, ACL, CVPR, EMNLP, IEEE SmartGridComm and other top conferences, as well as IEEE TMC, TII, TSG, TPWRS, TTE and other leading journals, and applies its results to smart grids and industrial large models. The lab works on three directions:

  • Large models (reasoning, prompt tuning, multimodal): RL-based reasoning and end-to-end decoding; prompt optimization and evaluation scheduling; agent skill retrieval and workflow design; visual chain-of-thought, video temporal grounding and visual attention in multimodal LLMs; retrieval-augmented generation, code completion, efficient mixture-of-experts and safety alignment.
  • Intelligent EV charging optimization: pricing and scheduling for charging and battery-swapping networks, station planning, and prospect-theoretic models of driver behavior; our algorithms support China's largest vehicle-grid interaction demonstration.
  • Federated learning: convergence and fairness theory; client sampling and adaptive aggregation; clustered federated learning under data heterogeneity and distribution shifts; personalized and decentralized federated learning; federated unlearning and incentive mechanisms.

Lab Related Reports

May 2026 Machine Heart GRPO Hits a Wall? G²RPO-A Gives Small Models a Reasoning Boost via Adaptive Guidance

T-lab proposes G²RPO-A, an adaptive-guidance variant of Group Relative Policy Optimization that injects structured external guidance into small-model RL training, substantially improving math and reasoning performance where vanilla GRPO plateaus. The paper is accepted to ACL 2026 Main Conference.

April 2026 Comprehensive Energy New Horizon IEEE TTE: When Is It Most Profitable to Upgrade a Charging Station to Fast Charging?

T-lab develops an economic-analysis framework for the optimal timing of upgrading regular charging stations to fast chargers, jointly modeling demand growth and upgrade cost to quantify the best decision window for operators under varying grid and traffic conditions. Published in IEEE Transactions on Transportation Electrification (TTE).

December 2025 Comprehensive Energy New Horizon IEEE IoTJ: Bounded Rationality in EV Highway Charging Decisions

T-lab proposes a bounded rationality Bayesian game framework, first applying prospect theory to EV highway charging decisions. Compared to traditional models, total social cost drops 44%, station daily procurement cost falls 29%, and peak queue length reduces up to 4x. Published in IEEE IoTJ (JCR Q1, IF 9.6).

November 2025 Machine Heart AutoDeco Tops HF Daily: Truly End-to-End LMs that End Manual Decoding

Proposed by Tencent AI Lab with CUHK-Shenzhen, AutoDeco equips Transformers with lightweight heads to dynamically predict temperature and top-p per token, and introduces a differentiable soft top-p to enable end-to-end training. It achieves SOTA-level results across models/tasks with ~1.7% latency overhead, and shows early signs of natural-language controllable decoding. See coverage for details.

August 2025 China News 4026 NEVs Become 'Power Banks' for the Grid

T-lab's research advances vehicle-to-grid synergy, enabling smart interactions between electric vehicles and the power grid. See the China News story for details.
